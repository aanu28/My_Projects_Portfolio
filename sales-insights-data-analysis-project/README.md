### Project Overview: Sales Insights for AtliQ Hardware

In this project, I analyzed sales data for **AtliQ Hardware**, a large computer hardware company in India, to build a scalable and decision-focused sales analytics solution.

I started by querying the transactional sales database using **SQL** to perform data exploration and analysis. This included identifying key entities such as customers, products, markets, and time periods, and deriving metrics like total revenue, sales quantity, and market-wise performance. SQL was also used to validate data consistency and extract clean, structured datasets for reporting.

The processed data was then imported into **Power BI**, where I carried out further data transformation using **Power Query**. This involved:
- Currency normalization to maintain consistent financial reporting  
- Removal of redundant and irrelevant fields  
- Data type validation and relationship modeling between fact and dimension tables  

I designed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** with a focus on usability and performance, enabling business users to drill down across markets, products, and time periods. Key KPIs such as revenue trends, sales volume, top-performing markets, and customer contributions were visualized to support quick, data-driven decision-making.

The final solution provides a consolidated view of the company’s sales performance and empowers leadership with actionable insights to effectively track growth and optimize business strategies.
